Tribute to Chris Brimelow

doctorwhoscarf.com was originally founded by Chris Brimelow in the early 2000s. He was an avid fan of color matching and detail accuracy, and in 2005, was invited by then Shada scarf owner Richard Baron Cohen to conduct some of the first fan examinations of the costume item. Chris would later go on to own the scarf himself, and continue to allow people to examine it, compare yarns to it, as well as pursue his desire for perfect color matches.

In 2013, Chris would cede control of doctorwhoscarf.com to Gene Fender. In 2017, Chris would also cede ownership of the Shada scarf to someone else, but he did not stop pursuing his dream. By this point, he was custom overdying his own yarn to dial in exact matches to the Shada scarf. After years of trial and error, in 2021, he would eventually accomplish color matches so close that in his own words, "as far as the colors go, I feel I can do very little else!" -(Brimelow).

Unfortunately for Fourth Doctor fans, people in the scarf community, and especially people who knew him, it would not be long after this, on June 5th, 2022, that Chris would die of cancer. Chris was beloved, respected, and will be sorely missed.

Rest in Peace.
